This 4-foot x 6-foot (1.3-m x 2.0-m) oil painting of the band, which the artist created as a donation to the Cal Marching Band, is installed in the band room on the UC Berkeley campus. The painting, which contains 15 figures, is titled *The Cal Band Relaxes in North Tunnel Before the Big Game*. Oil on Canvas, 2013. Shown here with details of the finished painting and the preparatory drawings behind it.
